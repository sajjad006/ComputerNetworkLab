# Build
make

# --- Live demo (slow, human-watchable trace) ---
# Terminal 1: start the channel server first.
#   args: <port> <N> <scheme> <p> <frameSlots> <jamSlots> <maxSlots> [demoDelayMs]
#   demoDelayMs > 0 prints a live per-slot trace and paces the loop for demo purposes.
./channel_server 9500 4 one_persistent 0.0 5 1 60 150

# Terminals 2-5 (or a loop in one terminal): one station process each.
#   args: <host> <port> <scheme> <p> <backoffCap>
./station 127.0.0.1 9500 one_persistent 0.0 10
./station 127.0.0.1 9500 one_persistent 0.0 10
./station 127.0.0.1 9500 one_persistent 0.0 10
./station 127.0.0.1 9500 one_persistent 0.0 10

# --- Same demo, other schemes (compare collision behavior side by side) ---

./channel_server 9503 4 csma_cd        0.0 5 1 60 150
